# TuneLoad

A simple, free music streaming and downloader app for Android.

TuneLoad streams millions of songs through YouTube Music — search anything, play full albums and playlists, and download tracks to listen offline, all in a clean dark UI.

> [!NOTE]
> TuneLoad is not affiliated with Google or YouTube in any way. Streams are powered by the YouTube Music catalog, and the app does not host or distribute any media content.

## Features

- 🎧 **Streaming** — Search and play songs, albums, playlists, and artists from YouTube Music
- ⬇️ **Downloads** — Download tracks for offline playback (saved to `Download/TuneLoad`)
- 🎶 **Your Library** — Liked songs, playlists, and albums synced from your YouTube Music account
- 📁 **Local Files** — Scan and play music stored on your device
- 📝 **Lyrics** — Synced and word-level karaoke-style lyrics
- 🌙 **Clean Dark UI** — Modern Material 3 design with fast skeleton loading
- 🤝 **Jams** — Real-time collaborative listening sessions with friends
